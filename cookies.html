Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Cookies are small files a website can store on your device. This page explains what this website does and does not use.

1. This website sets no cookies

  • No tracking or advertising cookies
  • No analytics (such as Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel)
  • No browser storage is used to remember you

Because nothing is stored on your device, there is no cookie banner and nothing for you to accept or reject.

2. Other companies

  • Google Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google's servers. Google states that the Fonts service does not set cookies, but it does see your IP address when your browser requests the fonts.
  • GitHub Pages: the hosting provider may record your IP address in server logs for security.
  • WhatsApp: if you click a WhatsApp button you leave this site, and WhatsApp's own cookie and privacy policies apply.

3. If this changes

If we ever add analytics or anything else that stores information on your device, we will update this page first and ask for your consent where it is required.
