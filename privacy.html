Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information I collect, why, and what your rights are. I am a freelance web designer based in Nepal, and I try to collect as little information as possible.

1. Who is responsible

Abhinav Sigdel, Kohalpur, Banke, Lumbini Province, Nepal, is responsible for the personal information described here.

2. What this website collects

  • No forms, accounts or logins. This website does not ask you to type in any personal information.
  • No cookies and no analytics. I do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels or analytics tools. See the Cookie Policy.
  • Demo websites. The demo projects linked from this site are fictional. Their forms and "order" buttons are for demonstration only and do not send or store anything you type.

3. Information you choose to give me

If you contact me by WhatsApp, phone or email, I receive what you send: usually your name, phone number or email address, and your message. If you become a client, I also receive the business information and content you provide for your website (for example your logo, photos, menu or product list).

4. How I use it

  • To reply to you and prepare a quotation or design sample
  • To build, launch and maintain your website
  • To send invoices and keep basic business records

I only use your information for the purpose you gave it to me, with your consent, as required by Nepal's Privacy Act, 2075 (2018). I do not sell your information and I do not use it for advertising.

5. Other companies involved

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web host, it may record technical details such as your IP address in its server logs.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on this site. Your browser requests the font files from Google, which means Google can see your IP address.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) opens only if you click a WhatsApp button. Messages you send there are covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • Domain and hosting companies, when I set up a website for a client, receive the details needed to register the domain or hosting in the client's name.

6. How long I keep it

I keep enquiries for up to 12 months. I keep client project files and payment records for as long as the project and any care plan last, and afterwards for as long as tax and business rules require. You can ask me to delete your information sooner.

7. Your rights

You can ask me to show you the personal information I hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Contact me using the details below and I will respond within 15 days.

8. Children

This website and my services are for businesses and adults. I do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

If I change how I handle information (for example, if I add a contact form or analytics), I will update this page and the date at the top.
